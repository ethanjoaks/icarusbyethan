Privacy policy · Effective November 12, 2026

Privacy, by purpose.

Icarus Channel Manager is a private owner-operated tool used by Ethan Oaks for the IcarusByEthan Etsy business. It connects to YouTube using the owner's Google authorization. This policy describes the private integration's data practices and this public informational website; public sign-ups are not offered.

Information and planned access

If the tool is authorized and operated, it may handle:

  • Google authorization and refresh tokens;
  • channel and video identifiers and related metadata;
  • owner-provided media files and upload metadata;
  • aggregate analytics for the authorized channel; and
  • service and error logs needed to operate and secure the tool.

The tool does not ask for Google passwords or private messages. Its intended scopes are narrowly described:

  • youtube.upload — upload content supplied and authorized by the owner;
  • youtube.readonly — read YouTube channel and video information needed for channel reporting and upload workflows;
  • yt-analytics.readonly — retrieve read-only aggregate analytics for the owner's channel.

The tool is not intended to access advertising accounts, revenue data, or other creators' channels. Google authorization may allow selection or grant broader access than one channel, so we do not represent that a token is technically limited to one channel. Use is intended only for the owner's selected channel and authorized tasks.

Use and sharing

Google API data is used only for authorized uploads, channel reporting, and service security. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train general-purpose AI models. Access is limited to Ethan Oaks and, only when necessary, personnel supporting security or legal obligations.

Data may be processed by Google and by infrastructure hosting or other authorized service providers, only to provide the service, support security, or meet legal obligations. Providers may process information under their own applicable terms and privacy practices. The public static website may be hosted by GitHub Pages; its hosting provider may receive ordinary request information such as IP address, user agent, and requested page, subject to its policies.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Any Google tokens used by the tool are intended to be stored privately on the service server, never in a public GitHub repository. Owner-provided upload files are not intended to be retained beyond operational need. Stored channel/video metadata and analytics reports will be refreshed or deleted within 30 days, consistent with YouTube policies. Authorization tokens are retained only while access remains authorized and necessary for the tool's operation. If authorization is disconnected or the owner requests deletion, stored authorization data and associated Google API data will be deleted as soon as possible and within 7 days.

Videos already uploaded to YouTube remain on YouTube unless the owner separately deletes them there. The tool cannot delete those videos on the owner's behalf under this policy.

Operational controls: The private client checks cache expiry and supports revocation and local deletion. A daily maintenance check refreshes authorization and verifies the selected channel; it does not publish videos.

Security and your choices

We limit access to the purposes above, but do not claim an independent security audit, particular encryption-at-rest controls, or guaranteed data residency. You can revoke the app's Google access at Google Account connections. You may also contact Ethan Oaks to request deletion or ask a privacy question: ethan.j.oaks0@gmail.com.

YouTube API Services

Icarus Channel Manager's planned use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. This service's handling of Google user data is also intended to comply with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Operator and updates

Ethan Oaks operates the tool for the IcarusByEthan Etsy shop. This policy may be updated as the tool is configured; material changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
