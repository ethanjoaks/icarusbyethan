Privacy Policy — IcarusByEthan Pin Publisher

This app connects to a single Pinterest account to publish pins for the IcarusByEthan Etsy shop (etsy.com/shop/IcarusByEthan). It accesses only that account's own boards, pins, and analytics via Pinterest's API. Tokens are stored securely and never shared or sold. To request data deletion, contact the shop owner through the Etsy shop page above.
